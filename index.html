Est. 2022 — New Delhi

Born
from the
Void

VÔID was founded in the margins of what fashion was supposed to look like. We were tired of clothing that shouted for attention while saying nothing of substance. So we built something else entirely.

Every piece begins with one question: what does this garment mean? Not what does it look like — what does it mean? That process of meaning-making is what separates VÔID from what came before.

We're not a streetwear brand. We're not a luxury brand. We exist in the space between — uncomfortable, deliberate, and honest.

Shop the Collection →
01

Radical Honesty

We don't make trend-led clothing. Each collection is built on a concept, a conflict, a contradiction worth exploring. If it doesn't mean something, it doesn't get made.

02

Deliberate Scarcity

We produce limited quantities by design. Not as a marketing tactic — because oversaturation cheapens meaning. If everyone has it, it says nothing.

03

Material Truth

We source materials that will outlast the season. Heavy canvas, structured cotton, raw denim — fabrics that age into something better. Clothing that commits.

"We exist to prove that clothes can be a form of resistance — against mediocrity, against noise, against the expectation that fashion must be comfortable."
